Under the hood

Under the hood, a full ERP system. On the outside, one “Accept” button.

Every order, customer and menu item is a record in an accounting system, not a message in a chat. So nothing gets lost, everything can be counted, and when you need inventory, finance or a second location, they are already there.

What is ERP?

One program instead of a notebook, spreadsheets and chat threads. Orders, customers, menu, money and staff live in one place, with history and reports. We use ERPNext, an open-source system used by tens of thousands of companies worldwide.

FAQ

Let’s clear things up.

What happens if the assistant doesn’t know the answer?+

It doesn’t make things up. It tells the customer it will check, pauses the conversation and shows you the chat marked “waiting for you”.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Customers only need Telegram, you and your staff only need a phone browser. The kitchen is comfortable on a tablet.

Can I change the menu and prices myself?+

Yes, in the dashboard. The assistant uses the new data right away, no retraining and no developer.

Will it work if I don’t run a café?+

The scheme is the same for any business where people write and order: rentals, shops, tours, services. Message us and we’ll look at your case.

Will WhatsApp be supported?+

Yes, WhatsApp support is in the works. Message us now and we’ll connect you among the first as soon as it opens.

How much does it cost?+

Depends on the volume and what you already have. Message us on Telegram, we’ll work it out in one conversation.
